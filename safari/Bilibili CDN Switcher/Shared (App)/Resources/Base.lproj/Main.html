BiBoost - Speedup for Bili

Smoother 4K playback on web bilibili for overseas users

Turn on the extension on iPad

  1. 1

    Tap the button below to open bilibili.com in Safari.

  2. 2

    Tap the Extensions icon on the right of the address bar, then tap Manage Extensions.

  3. 3

    Turn on BiBoost - Speedup for Bili, then tap ✓ to finish.

  4. 4

    Tap the Extensions icon again, tap BiBoost, and choose Always Allow. Reload the page and you're done.

You can also turn it on in Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.

Turn on the extension on iPhone

  1. 1

    Open the Settings app and go to Apps › Safari › Extensions.

  2. 2

    Tap BiBoost - Speedup for Bili and turn on Allow Extension.

  3. 3

    Under website permissions, set bilibili.com to Allow.

  4. 4

    Tap the button below to open bilibili.com in Safari. Reload the page and you're done.

On iOS 17 or earlier, the path is Settings › Safari › Extensions. You can also tap Manage Extensions in Safari's page menu.

Turn on the extension on Mac

Turn on BiBoost in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

BiBoost is on. You can turn it off in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

BiBoost is off. Follow the steps below to turn it on.

  1. 1

    Click the button below. This app quits and Safari Settings opens to Extensions.

  2. 2

    Select the checkbox next to BiBoost - Speedup for Bili.

  3. 3

    Open bilibili.com in Safari, click the BiBoost icon in the toolbar, and choose Always Allow on This Website.
